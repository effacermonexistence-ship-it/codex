## What v144 fixes

Found by the v143 live red-team (case 11: "actually can we do the 13th instead" after the four-field checkpoint at 4pm; 38 s into the generic recovery ask, then "4pm" 72 s into the which-field ask). The closed-transition contract already treats a replaced date as invalidating its sibling time and routes to the time ask, but the durable time survived in state. The runner's funnel stage therefore still read "ready for the checkpoint" and authored the four-field block under the time-ask route (rejected: time cannot skip to double check), the model did the same, and structured-output contract v2 rejected every time re-ask, the recovery packet included, as re-asking a known field. The contradiction existed on v142 as well; the v142 red-team had been staged but never fired.

1. A date-only revision at the open checkpoint clears the sibling time in state, so route, runner and contracts agree: ask the time for the new date, then the next time answer re-issues the corrected checkpoint deterministically. A revision carrying its own time keeps it.
2. The runner never authors the four-field checkpoint under a post-form ask route (time, identity, availability); the controller's ask route wins.
3. Structured-output contract v3: after a live-turn checkpoint invalidation or a superseded checkpoint, the locked post-form route owns the latched booking fields, so a legitimate re-ask is never rejected as re-asking a known field.

Everything in v143 (checkpoint side question, await recovery gate, ready-identity rebase, executed-checkpoint state, scheduling guard, state-grounded greeting rule) carries over unchanged. Versions: structured-output contract v3, hard harness lock v160, divergence harness v5.
